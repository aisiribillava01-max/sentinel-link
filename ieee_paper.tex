\begin{abstract}
Along remote, high-altitude border perimeter zones spanning over 15,106 km of rugged mountainous terrain, forward military and paramilitary detachments operate under extreme operational friction characterized by non-line-of-sight (NLOS) topography, complete cellular infrastructure denial, and vulnerability to adversary Signal Intelligence (SIGINT) emitter triangulation. Traditional border surveillance architectures suffer from excessive false-alarm rates triggered by alpine wildlife, while forward soldiers operating in communication blackouts face delayed medical evacuation during casualties. Concurrently, unauthorized commercial smartphone usage by isolated personnel poses catastrophic operational security risks by leaking geospatial metadata to hostile electronic warfare interceptors. To overcome this trilemma, this paper presents Sentinel Link, an integrated, three-tier cyber-physical defence architecture: (1) an edge-deployed deep learning vision pipeline (YOLOv8 TensorRT INT8) mounted on ruggedized surveillance towers that achieves 94.6\% threat precision, successfully suppressing 96.2\% of non-threatening mammalian fauna false alarms in real-time thermal/infrared video feeds; (2) an offline-first, decentralized sub-GHz (865--867 MHz Indian ISM band) LoRa ad-hoc multi-hop mesh network integrated with a mil-spec biometric soldier wearable, guaranteeing an end-to-end alert latency of 1.42 seconds ($< 2.0$ s specification target) with autonomous self-healing rerouting under node jamming; and (3) a cryptographic air-gapped data diode policy engine operating at the Battalion Command post that scrubs 100\% of spatial, elevation, radio-frequency, and unit metadata before publishing verified welfare heartbeats to civilian families. Empirical hardware benchmarking, latency measurements, and mathematical security verifications demonstrate that Sentinel Link establishes resilient forward perimeter awareness and soldier casualty extraction while guaranteeing zero spatial leakage to external networks.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The defence of national land borders across remote mountainous theatres presents extraordinary terrain and communication challenges. Sentry posts, isolated observation points, and forward reconnaissance patrols operate in severe climatic conditions characterized by sub-zero ambient temperatures ($-20^\circ$C to $-40^\circ$C), high altitude ($>3,000$ m MSL), and deep glacial ravines that cause complete blockage of commercial terrestrial cellular (4G/5G) and line-of-sight UHF/VHF signals.

Under these conditions, three interconnected vulnerabilities persist:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Perimeter Surveillance Blind Spots and Sentry Fatigue:} Conventional optical cameras cannot penetrate Himalayan blizzards, dense mountain fog, or nighttime darkness. While Long-Wave Infrared (LWIR) thermal sensors detect thermal emissions, conventional motion-detection software triggers frequent false alarms due to the movement of native high-altitude mammalian fauna (such as the Himalayan ibex, snow leopards, brown bears, and yaks), leading to severe sentry desensitization.
    \item \textbf{Casualty Evacuation (CASEVAC) Delays in Denied Terrain:} Forward patrol units navigating hazardous crevasses are vulnerable to sudden hypothermia, acute mountain sickness (AMS), cardiac stress, and ballistic trauma. Without localized infrastructure, injured soldiers cannot broadcast casualty status, drastically exceeding the critical medical ``Golden Hour''.
    \item \textbf{The Tactical Communication Security (COMSEC) Paradox:} Soldiers deployed in remote outposts often attempt to utilize civilian smartphones or commercial satellite messengers to reassure anxious families. However, modern adversary Electronic Support Measures (ESM) and Signals Intelligence (SIGINT) systems exploit radio-frequency (RF) handshakes and unencrypted baseband packets, triangulating soldier positions with sub-50-meter accuracy to coordinate artillery or drone strikes.
\end{enumerate}

To fundamentally solve these issues without compromising operational security, this paper presents \textbf{Sentinel Link}: an end-to-end, indigenous, hardware-validated tactical architecture designed under the \textit{Atmanirbhar Bharat} and \textit{Innovations for Defence Excellence (iDEX)} defence frameworks.

\section{Related Work}
\subsection{Edge-AI Vision for Border Surveillance}
Vignesh et al. \cite{vignesh2023} evaluated lightweight convolutional neural networks on embedded platforms for automated thermal perimeter monitoring. Their findings showed that while infrared thermal imagery provides invariant detection of metabolic human heat signatures ($36.5^\circ$C -- $37.5^\circ$C) against frigid environmental backgrounds, standard bounding-box detectors misclassify large quadrupeds as crouching human combatants. 

Chen et al. \cite{chen2023} implemented YOLOv7 on unmanned aerial vehicles for border surveillance, establishing that TensorRT 8-bit integer (INT8) quantization reduces inference latency by up to 62\% without significant mean Average Precision (mAP) degradation. Sentinel Link builds upon this foundation by integrating a specialized spatial-temporal locomotion aspect ratio filter on YOLOv8n to suppress wildlife false alarms at the edge compute node.

\subsection{Tactical LoRa Networks and Wearable Sensors}
Ramkumar et al. \cite{ramkumar2022} demonstrated wearable Photoplethysmography (PPG) sensing utilizing the MAX30102 sensor for real-time soldier heart rate and peripheral capillary oxygen saturation ($SpO_2$) extraction. However, their system utilized GSM/GPRS modems, rendering it completely inoperative in non-cellular tactical frontiers.

Centenaro et al. \cite{centenaro2021} investigated multi-hop LoRa topologies, confirming that sub-GHz frequencies (specifically 865--867 MHz in India) exhibit superior diffraction around rocky ridges and mountain defilades compared to 2.4 GHz protocols. However, conventional LoRaWAN adopts a single-hop star topology that collapses if the central gateway is disabled. Sentinel Link incorporates an autonomous ad-hoc distance-vector routing protocol capable of dynamic rerouting around jammed or destroyed relay nodes.

\subsection{SIGINT Vulnerabilities and Air-Gap Security}
Sharma et al. \cite{sharma2022} documented emitter triangulation methodologies employed by modern electronic warfare units, illustrating that civilian GSM smartphones emit continuous periodic registration beacons that reveal squad locations. Rosen and Sterling \cite{rosen2022} analyzed unidirectional data diodes for cross-domain military information exchange. Sentinel Link adapts this cryptographic air-gap principle to decouple classified front-line tactical telemetry from unclassified civilian family welfare notifications.

\section{Problem Formulation \& Mathematical Model}
\subsection{False-Alarm Probability Model}
Let a thermal surveillance frame $F_t$ contain candidate detected bounding boxes $B = \{b_1, b_2, \dots, b_k\}$. Each detection $b_i$ is characterized by a confidence score $c_i \in [0, 1]$, an aspect ratio $\alpha_i = \frac{w_i}{h_i}$, and a class label $L_i \in \{\text{human}, \text{uav}, \text{fauna}\}$.

In Sentinel Link, a secondary heuristic classifier $\mathcal{H}(b_i)$ is applied:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{H}(b_i) = \begin{cases} 
\text{DISMISS}, & \text{if } \alpha_i > 1.25 \text{ and } L_i = \text{fauna} \\
\text{ESCALATE}, & \text{if } L_i \in \{\text{human}, \text{uav}\} \text{ and } c_i \ge \theta_{th}
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
This suppresses quadruped fauna triggers while ensuring that the probability of detection $P_D$ for armed bipedal silhouettes remains above 94\%.

\subsection{End-to-End Latency Formulation}
The total latency $T_{\text{latency}}$ from sensor detection or soldier SOS initiation to Battalion Command Post (C2) alert presentation is modeled as:
\begin{equation}
T_{\text{latency}} = T_{\text{infer}} + \sum_{j=1}^{N_{\text{hops}}} \left( T_{\text{air}, j} + T_{\text{proc}, j} \right) + T_{\text{C2}}
\end{equation}
Where $T_{\text{infer}}$ is the edge neural network inference time on the Jetson Orin Nano ($26.3$ ms). For a 36-byte packed binary payload at $SF = 9$, $BW = 125$ kHz, $T_{\text{air}} \approx 184$ ms per hop. Across a 3-hop topology, the total airtime is $\approx 552$ ms, ensuring $T_{\text{latency}} < 1.5$ s, safely satisfying our $<2.0$ s specification.

\subsection{Zero-Leakage Privacy Formulation}
Let the classified soldier telemetry frame at the Battalion Command Post be $\mathcal{T}_{\text{tactical}}$ containing exact MGRS coordinates and elevation. Let the egress mapping function be $\Phi: \mathcal{T}_{\text{tactical}} \to \mathcal{M}_{\text{civilian}}$. The spatial leakage metric $\mathcal{L}_{\text{leak}}$ is defined as mutual information:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\text{leak}} = I(\text{Coordinates}; \mathcal{M}_{\text{civilian}}) = 0\text{ bits}
\end{equation}
This guarantees mathematical zero-knowledge transfer of soldier geographic location to public networks.

\section{System Architecture \& Implementation}
\subsection{Edge-AI Surveillance Mast (Tier 1)}
The edge vision system couples an uncooled LWIR thermal imager ($8$--$14\ \mu$m) and an optical camera directly to an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano (8GB) compute module. The incoming stream is quantized to TensorRT INT8 precision and evaluated through custom-trained YOLOv8n weights. When candidate motion is identified:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Quadruped fauna geometry auto-suppresses alarm sirens.
    \item Armed human silhouettes or low-RCS rotary drones trigger a compressed binary incursion frame passed via UART to a Semtech SX1262 LoRa transceiver.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Soldier Wearable \& LoRa Mesh (Tier 2)}
The wearable unit is engineered around an Espressif ESP32-S3 microcontroller:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Biometric Telemetry:} Analog Devices MAX30102 PPG sensor tracks continuous heart rate and $SpO_2$.
    \item \textbf{Inertial Shock \& Fall Engine:} Bosch BMA400 3-axis accelerometer detects impacts ($>3.8g$) followed by immobility to trigger autonomous Man-Down casualty alerts.
    \item \textbf{One-Press Tactile SOS:} Dedicated hardware interrupt button for instant manual casualty beaconing.
    \item \textbf{Stealth/Blackout Mode:} Cuts off periodic RF beacons and switches OLED to night-vision red phosphor.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Command Center (C2) \& Air-Gap Sanitizer (Tier 3)}
The C2 Command Dashboard features a 20 km polar radar sweep canvas and human-in-the-loop verification dialogs. The Air-Gap Policy Sanitizer strips 100\% of spatial and radio metadata before publishing verified status pings to family portals.

\section{Experimental Results}
\subsection{Edge-AI Detection and Fauna Suppression}
Across 2,400 benchmark frames, Sentinel Link achieved \textbf{94.6\% threat precision} at a confidence threshold of 0.75, successfully surpassing the $>92\%$ project specification, while filtering \textbf{96.2\%} of mammalian wildlife traversals. Average inference latency was measured at \textbf{26.3 ms per frame} ($38$ FPS) on the Jetson Orin Nano.

\subsection{Mesh Latency and Self-Healing}
In physical multi-hop simulations, end-to-end latency over 1 hop measured $0.24$ s, 2 hops measured $0.81$ s, and 3 hops measured \textbf{1.42 s} ($99.2\%$ Packet Delivery Ratio). Under simulated electronic jamming of Repeater 2, the self-healing routing protocol converged and rerouted packets through Repeater 3 within \textbf{420 ms} with zero packet loss.

\subsection{Air-Gap Security Audit}
Across 1,000 simulated family relay transmissions, automated packet inspection scripts verified that \textbf{0 bytes} of GPS coordinates, MGRS grids, elevation, or RF carrier frequencies were leaked.

\section{Conclusion}
Sentinel Link provides an indigenous solution to border blind spots, soldier communication blackouts, and civilian phone signal leaks. By fusing edge-AI thermal detection, an offline self-healing LoRa mesh network, and a cryptographic air-gap relay, the platform guarantees immediate tactical casualty response while safeguarding troop locations. Future extensions include micro-UAV aerial mesh relays and quantum-resistant lattice-based cryptography.
